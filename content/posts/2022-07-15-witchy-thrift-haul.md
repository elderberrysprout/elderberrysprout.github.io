<p>Short and sweet, how tasty is that!?</p>
<p>The only thing I want to link for your reference is the Tilly and the Buttons <a href="https://www.tillyandthebuttons.com/2015/07/how-to-make-eye-mask-free-pattern.html">sleep mask</a> pattern if you want to make your own sleep mask with an embroidered eye on it!
