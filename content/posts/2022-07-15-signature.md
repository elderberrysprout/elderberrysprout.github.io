<p>Hi everybody, I have recently filled up the magic signature in my travelers notebook and I now need to make another one. This one I made from an assortment of meaningful papers and you can see the process in the video below!</p>
<p>This signature is what I will use to write all my spiritual notes in. I know I remember things better when I write them down! This is also where I will journal about triggers, diving deep into them to find the roots. All of the larger pages are cut down to 8 1/2” by 10 7/8” so that they fit in my journal well. Some of the smaller pages didn’t need to be trimmed at all! All these pages are kept in place in my travelers notebook only by the elastic and that is enough! </p>
<h3>Papers Used</h3>
<ol><li><p>page from an art book, torn on edges</p></li><li><p>orange cardstock (as the cover)</p></li><li><p>80s copy paper</p></li><li><p>college ruled notebook paper</p></li><li><p>vintage wrapping paper</p></li><li><p>small page from a vintage book</p></li><li><p>printer paper</p></li><li><p>beige craft paper</p></li><li><p>small gold leafed paper</p></li><li><p>ivy decorated napkin</p></li><li><p>beige craft paper</p></li><li><p>coloring book page of a wolf</p><p><br/></p></li></ol>
<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/assets/images/1634780830918-bm9svh3y3q8yege139mr_giphy.gif"/>
<p>Now this is just an assortment of what I had access to and what I wanted to focus on in the next weeks. If you want to buy pretty papers, or print out pages, that is an option as well! The world is your playground when you start making your own travelers notebook signatures! A good rule of thumb for journal signatures you want to write in alot is to use mainly plain papers that can be written on. I know I always want to add super fun patterned papers to my signatures but then they don’t function the way i need them to. Keep in mind your journals use when you are constructing it. Also an easy way to make your paper more exciting is to tea/coffee dye them! Have fun and experiment!</p>
<p>If you want more inspiration for making junk journals, I would suggest checking out <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?ab_channel=TreasureBooks&amp;v=xSegjt4aVto">Treasure Books</a>, <a href="https://www.youtube.com/c/releasethecraftin">Release the Craftin</a>,<a href="https://www.youtube.com/c/PinkOddBird"> Pink odd Bird</a>, <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCSkwO0IRem3AAzRvMj6jPbw">The Paper Outpost</a> for more resources! I Hope this helps to inspire you to make a signature of your own from scrap paper!</p>
<p>And so it is!</p>
