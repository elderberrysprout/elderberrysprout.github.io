<p>I have been really focused on increasing the functionality of my travelers notebook in the beginning of this year. With that I have seen a couple of holes in functionality that I wanted to remedy in my Travelers Notebook this week. You can see the alterations in the video below!</p>
<h3>The Modifications</h3>
<p>I made 3 modifications to this travelers notebook, two of them are rather simple and the other one (the folder) has a lot of steps to get the final product. </p>
<ol><li><p>Mindfulness Charm</p></li><li><p>Large Copper Paperclips</p></li><li><p>DIY Folder with a permanent Pen Loop</p></li></ol>
<h3>Mindfulness Charm</h3>
<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/assets/images/1644618582185-mnhcpdx4tju7ybdhow3a_charm-travelers-notebook-modifications.png"/>
<p>This charm was made by gluing a pressed penny to a piece of leather. Then I cut out the leather in the shape of a UFO, just like the pressed penny I used!</p>
<p>I made this charm to remind me that I am a part of something larger than myself every time I look at my travelers notebook. This helps me not to take my problems too seriously! </p>
<h3>Copper Paperclips</h3>
<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/assets/images/1644618729375-jockyl54jileh97qxp46_paperclipo-and-charm-travelers-notebook-modifications.png"/>
<p><br/>I used 1ft strips of copper wire and I bent the wire with some pliers and my fingers into a classic paperclip shape and a paperclip flag shape! With doing this, there are really so many options for shaping. I thought to make an arrow, or a mushroom, Ahhh the options are endless, however I don’t need that many paperclips, so I stopped after the first two. </p>
<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/assets/images/1644620372126-mgw82mnp15ay30ikecra_elderberrysprout---printable-paperclip-pattern-free.png"/>
<p>Download your free copper paperclip bending template <a href="/assets/files/ElderberrySprout-Printable-Paperclip-Pattern-FREE.pdf">HERE</a>. This is what the pattern looks like if you want to make them without the pattern :) They are about 3” tall and 1” wide. </p>
<h3>Folder Dashboard with Pen Loop</h3>
<p>I started with a hanging file folder to make this. Using this will make the final folder lice and rigid. These I found at the thrift store for 10 cents a piece, such a steal!</p>
<p>I used a TN folder that I like the size of to help me draft the pattern I want.  It is just a hair bigger than the size of the papers in my signature so that they are protected on the edges. </p>
<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/assets/images/1644614472595-ee7kp0zz3bsrq6i3a1c9_folder-dashboard-pattern.png"/>
<p>Pattern for the TN Folder</p>
<p>Then I cut the folder out and folded it using scisors as a bone folder to get crisp edges. After that I glued the flaps together and voila!</p>
<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/assets/images/1644614943916-1csq2hq51cyt0fexanck_folder-dashboard.png"/>
<p>Diy Folder after gluing </p>
<p>Then I used a scrap of soft leather and some cotton floss to stitch a pen loop onto the back flap of the folder. I used a thick and strong needle to punch through this folder material and the leather and it worked really well! I cut the leather so that the loop would fit my favorite fountain pen perfectly and it does!</p>
<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/assets/images/1644622302769-c0ms60dm5wcvbs7ilp01_diy-folder-dashboard-filled-uptravelers-notebook-modifications.png"/>
<p>Filled up folder with slits in the pockets for drafting rulers!</p>
<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/assets/images/1644618846739-84yhzd0sgd0o3xtm6jzp_diy-folder-penloop-travelers-notebook-modifications.png"/>
<p>The leather pen loop installed on the back of my new folder, such pretty stitching!</p>
<p>After the folder was all stitched up, I installed all of my new modifications!</p>

<h3>All Together</h3>
<p>I love these modifications. I even think that I will make another folder with a pen loop to cover another one of my signatures! I like to use multiple fountain pens so that would ensure that my all my pens don’t get yanked off of my TN! I also want to make more paperclips but I don’t know what I will do with them so ill have to come up with some other uses for these paperclips :)</p>
<p>Thank you so much for reading! I hope this inspired you to create some Travelers Notebook modifications that will increase the functionality of your own Travelers Notebook!</p>
