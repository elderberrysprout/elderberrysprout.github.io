<p>Fill up your own cup with these self love themed beeswax chalice candles. Each beeswax candle is lovingly poured into a vintage silver plated chalice and is dressed with dried rose petals from my mothers garden.</p>
<p>These candles are designed to be altar decor. They can be burned, but will not burn evenly due to the abnormal chalice shaping and the rose petal dressing. If you do decide to burn this decoration, for the most even burn you must remove the rose petals and do not burn for more than 2 hours at a time. The C style chalice candles I expect to burn the best of this collection. </p>
<p>Due to the nature of this product do expect variability and slight defects not shown in the pictures. Vintage finds are all so unique! </p>
<p>Made in Eastern WA with local sustainable beeswax.</p>
<p>Beeswax is one of the cleanest burning waxes around. It is said to give off negative ions which help to purify the air wherever you are burning it.</p>
<p>I  am committed to using zero waste practices in shipping, so all  packaging will be made from 100% recyclable and composable materials. In addition I use recycled and 2nd hand packaging materials as frequently as I can source them. This way I can create one of a kind beautifully and sustainably packaged  shipments special for you!</p>
