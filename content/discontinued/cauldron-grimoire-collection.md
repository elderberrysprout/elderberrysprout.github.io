<p>This is a collection of 4 grimoires. They each have their own personality and variation.</p>
<p>Full flip through video - https://youtu.be/0YzszFriZFI</p>
<p>There are 20 sheets in each journal (40 pages total) so they would work well for an October daily journaling practice! I may use one myself!</p>
<p>The front inside cover has a cauldron pattern, and the majority of the pages are hand dyed paper in a mystical bohemian color pallett. The cover is a patchwork brocade fabric with gild and silver stitch work!</p>
<p>The journals measure roughly 9.5x6</p>
