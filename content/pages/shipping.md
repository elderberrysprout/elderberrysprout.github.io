## How long does my order take to ship?

I can process orders and send them out generally in 3–5 days. Then I’ll hand your package to the USPS and they’ll take it from there. I will send you a tracking code so you can keep an eye on your shipment.

## Do you offer international shipping?

I set out the intention to sell my wares locally to try to generate sustainable community and economy where I live. I am in Washington state in the USA so I limit the range of my sales to within the USA.

## What is your return policy?

If products are damaged upon arrival I am happy to accept returns. Due to the CO2 emissions associated with returning products, I would like to avoid it as much as I can. There are variations in the products since they are handmade, so you may not receive the exact candle you see in the photos. If you have any questions before purchasing please [reach out](/contact/).
