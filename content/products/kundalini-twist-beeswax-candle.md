<p>Much like the Kundalini serpent of energy traveling up the body this candle spirals, seeking balance in the center of the candle. This candle can be used for many rituals and is best used to generate balance and energy in ones life. This candle is made of 2 candles twisting around one another. This means you can also use it in love spells or self integration spellwork.</p>
<p>Product Specifications</p>
<p>Height: 9in tall</p>
<p>Diameter: 7/8 inch</p>
<p>Burn Time: Dependent on burn conditions 4-6hrs</p>
<p>Wick trimming length: 1/4 inch (optional)</p>
<p>Caution</p>
<p>*Do not leave candles unattended</p>
<p>*use caution when extinguishing the flame</p>
<p>*trim wick to 1/4" for safest burn</p>
<p>For the Best Burn</p>
<p>*Burn in draft free room</p>
<p>*use correct candle holder</p>
<p>*supervise wick, straightening and trimming when needed.</p>
<p>These candles are hand dipped! This process takes a long time and requires a wick to be submerged into a hot bath of liquid beeswax numerous times, slowly building up the candle layer by layer. Due to the nature of this process, expect slight variations from candle to candle.</p>
