<p>This pair of large taper candles looks lovely on a formal dining table, great for romantic dinners, holidays or just a night to treat yourself. The lovely aroma of beeswax heating up can be enjoyed as you nibble your meal.</p>
<p>The price listed is for one pair of candles. Each candle will burn 9-10 hrs each.</p>
<p>The candles measure approximately 10”x7/8”. Made in Eastern WA with local sustainable beeswax.</p>
<p>These candles are hand dipped! This process takes a long time and requires a wick to be submerged into a hot bath of liquid beeswax numerous times, slowly building up the candle layer by layer. Due to the nature of this process there will be slight variations from candle to candle.</p>
<p>I am committed to using zero waste practices in shipping, so all packaging will be made from 100% recycled and compostable materials. I will use these materials to make a one of a kind beautifully packaged shipments special for you!</p>
<p>Caution</p>
<p>*Do not leave candles unattended</p>
<p>*use caution when extinguishing the flame</p>
<p>*trim wick to 1/4" for safest burn</p>
