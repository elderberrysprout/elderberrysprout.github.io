<p>These 4.5” birthday candles are a great way to celebrate a birthday! just poke them in a cake or a muffin and make a wish! Plus the beeswax is completely edible so you can trust that there are no chemicals dripping into your delicious frosting!</p>
<p>Candles burn for 30mins. May they bring light and help you celebrate another year on this planet!</p>
<p>The candles measure approximately 4.5” tall.</p>
<p>These candles are hand dipped! This process takes a long time and requires a wick to be submerged into a hot bath of liquid beeswax numerous times, slowly building up the candle layer by layer.</p>
<p>Made in Eastern WA with local sustainable beeswax.</p>
<p>I am committed to using zero waste practices in shipping, so all packaging will be made from 100% recycled and compostable materials. I will use these materials to make a one of a kind beautifully packaged shipments special for you!</p>
