<p>Chime candles are small candles that are often used in spell work, for meditation purposes because they will fully burn up in a couple of hours. Just set an intention and let it burn until it self extinguishes. These candles also fit in traditional Swedish chime candle holders. They can be a perfect edition to the mantle for a yuletide celebration.</p>
<p>These candles fit perfectly in traditional Swedish Angel Chime candle holders.</p>
<p>This product listing is for 9 chime candles. </p>
<p>These candles are hand dipped! This process takes a long time and requires a wick to be submerged into a hot bath of liquid beeswax numerous times, slowly building up the candle layer by layer. Due to the nature of this process there will be slight variation from candle to candle.</p>
<p>The candles measure approximately 4.5”x1/2”. Made in Eastern WA with local sustainable beeswax.</p>
<p>I am committed to using zero waste practices in shipping, so all packaging will be made from 100% recyclable and compostable materials. I will use these materials to make a one of a kind beautifully packaged shipments special for you!</p>
<p>Caution</p>
<p>*Do not leave candles unattended</p>
<p>*use caution when extinguishing the flame</p>
<p>*trim wick to 1/4" for safest burn</p>
