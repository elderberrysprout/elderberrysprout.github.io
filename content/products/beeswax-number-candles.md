<p>Hand-poured beeswax candles to help you celebrate those special moments. Use for a birthday or an anniversary! The best part is the non-toxic beeswax is safe to be in contact with your delicious cakes and frosting. So if the candle drips on your cake, there are no worries!</p>
<p>One order is for one number candle. If you need multiples, select the needed numbers individually.</p>
<p>This candle due to its design burns for 15-25 minutes. When fully burned you will have a keepsake piece of beeswax. You can keep this for posterity or you may use this chunk of beeswax to wax things around your house (zippers, drawers) or you can use it as an ingredient in self care products! </p>
<p>These  candles are hand poured! Due to the nature of this  process, expect slight variations from candle to candle.</p>
<p>Made in Eastern WA with local sustainable beeswax.</p>
<p>Beeswax is one of the cleanest burning waxes around. It is said to give off negative ions which help to purify the air wherever you are burning it. </p>
<p>I  am committed to using zero waste practices in shipping, so all  packaging will be made from 100% recyclable and biodegradable materials. In addition I use recycled and 2nd hand packaging materials as frequently as I can source them. This way I can create one of a kind beautifully and sustainably packaged  shipments special for you!</p>
<p>Caution</p>
<ul><li><p>Never leave burning candle unattended</p></li><li><p>Keep out of reach of children and pets</p></li><li><p>Burn in draft free room</p></li><li><p>Use caution when extinguishing flame</p></li><li><p>Trim wick to 1/4" for safest burn</p></li></ul>
